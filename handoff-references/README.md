# Visual reference priority

These eight images describe the intended result. They are visual references,
not validated runtime sprite atlases. Checkerboard or white backgrounds may be
baked in; validate real alpha on generated assets.

1. `naruto-rasengan-style-reference.png`: sole Naruto character-appearance authority for detailed, crisp arcade pixel style, proportions, shading, and pose progression. Runtime Naruto is on the RIGHT facing LEFT.
2. `sasuke-chidori-style-reference.png`: sole Sasuke character-appearance authority for detailed, crisp arcade pixel style, proportions, shading, and pose progression. Runtime Sasuke is on the LEFT facing RIGHT.
3. `street-fighter-layout-reference.png`: arena framing, fighter scale, top names and mirrored HP. Keep THREE HP segments, no timer, score system or free-movement fighting controls.
4. `valley-environment-reference.png`: statues and waterfall composition. Match the detailed arcade pixel treatment; fighters stand ON the water surface, not on a platform.
5. `gold-buttons-reference.png`: gold beveled pixel-button treatment. Play, Sound, Back and Replay only, with interaction states.
6. `title-logo-reference.png`: title gag. Keep NARUTO, cross out SHIPPUDEN with a rough red X, add handwritten DATTEBAYO beneath it.
7. `battle-layout-reference.png`: lower HUD placement: camera left, compact title center, RPS right. Replace RPS with current sign/tutorial while casting. Street Fighter framing remains the arena layout authority.
8. `secret-ending-composition-reference.png`: collapse on rocks, non-graphic injured arms implied/obscured. Generate a new matching detailed arcade pixel-art ending, do not display this screenshot as finished art.

The two character style references are the sole appearance authority. The
remaining six images control composition and UI decisions as listed above.
`source-reference/` contains only legacy prototype implementation and audio
references; it has no authority over character appearance or art style. No
private chat screenshots are included. References establish no license grant.
